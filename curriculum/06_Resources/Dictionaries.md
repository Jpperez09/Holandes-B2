# Dictionaries and Corpora

## Purpose

Curated, evaluated catalogue of Dutch dictionaries, corpus tools, frequency lists, and reference databases for Juanpa's B2 path. Each entry includes name, link, level, use case, strengths, weaknesses, cost, and Juanpa-specific usage notes.

---

## A. Online Dictionaries — Monolingual (Dutch → Dutch)

### Van Dale Online — *vandale.nl*

- **Link**: https://www.vandale.nl
- **Level**: B1+ (definitions in Dutch).
- **Use case**: Authoritative Dutch dictionary — the *OED* of Dutch.
- **Strengths**: Definitions, usage labels, register marking, examples; *de/het* always given; collocations; etymology in the paid version.
- **Weaknesses**: Free tier only gives short definitions and a translation popup; full dictionary requires subscription.
- **Cost**: Free (limited) / **paid** (~€10/month) for the *Groot Woordenboek*.
- **For Juanpa**: Use the **free public lookup** from A2 onward as the canonical "this is the Dutch definition" reference. Subscribe only at B1+ when monolingual lookups become routine.

### Woorden.org

- **Link**: https://www.woorden.org
- **Level**: A2+.
- **Use case**: Free monolingual Dutch dictionary; simpler than Van Dale.
- **Strengths**: Free, plain-language definitions, includes *de/het*, examples, conjugations, sometimes audio.
- **Weaknesses**: Smaller corpus than Van Dale; ads.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Default monolingual dictionary in the A2–B1 phase; faster and gentler than Van Dale.

### Algemeen Nederlands Woordenboek (ANW) — *anw.ivdnt.org*

- **Link**: https://anw.ivdnt.org
- **Level**: B2+.
- **Use case**: Scientific dictionary of contemporary Dutch produced by the *Instituut voor de Nederlandse Taal* (INT).
- **Strengths**: Linguistically rich entries, corpus-based examples, semantic relations.
- **Weaknesses**: Not exhaustive (still being built); academic register.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Reach-tool at B1→B2 when nuance matters; useful for understanding semantic networks.

### Etymologiebank — *etymologiebank.nl*

- **Link**: https://etymologiebank.nl
- **Level**: any (for curiosity).
- **Use case**: Etymology of Dutch words.
- **Strengths**: Free, comprehensive, shows Germanic/Latin origins.
- **Weaknesses**: Reading entries requires intermediate Dutch.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Useful occasional sanity-check on whether a word is Germanic (likely cognate with English) or Latinate.

## B. Online Dictionaries — Bilingual

### Wiktionary (Dutch language portal)

- **Link**: https://nl.wiktionary.org and https://en.wiktionary.org
- **Level**: any.
- **Use case**: Free crowdsourced multilingual dictionary.
- **Strengths**: Free; gender (*de/het*), plural, full conjugation table, IPA, audio for many words, English/Spanish translations side-by-side, etymology, declension of adjectives.
- **Weaknesses**: Coverage uneven; rare words sparse.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: **Default bilingual lookup** A0–B1; the side-by-side EN/NL/ES is uniquely useful.

### Glosbe NL–EN / NL–ES

- **Link**: https://glosbe.com
- **Level**: A1–B2.
- **Use case**: Translation + many example sentences from parallel corpora.
- **Strengths**: Free, large example sentence corpus.
- **Weaknesses**: Example sentences sometimes machine-aligned; quality varies; ads.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Use specifically when you need **context examples** of a word, not for the headword definition.

### Linguee (DeepL) NL ↔ EN

- **Link**: https://www.linguee.com/dutch-english
- **Level**: A2–C1.
- **Use case**: Parallel-corpus translations from real published bilingual texts (EU, news, websites).
- **Strengths**: High-quality real-world example pairs; very useful for translation choices and idiomatic preposition use.
- **Weaknesses**: No NL ↔ ES pair (must go via EN).
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Best resource for figuring out *which* of several translations is natural in context.

### Reverso Context NL ↔ EN / ES

- **Link**: https://context.reverso.net
- **Level**: A2+.
- **Use case**: Crowd + ML translated context examples.
- **Strengths**: Both NL-EN and NL-ES; many examples.
- **Weaknesses**: Many examples come from movie subtitles or auto-translated content; quality lower than Linguee.
- **Cost**: Free / paid (ad removal).
- **For Juanpa**: Useful for NL-ES pairing where Linguee has nothing.

### DeepL Translator

- **Link**: https://www.deepl.com
- **Level**: any.
- **Use case**: Sentence/paragraph translation.
- **Strengths**: Best-in-class MT quality for NL↔EN; supports NL↔ES.
- **Weaknesses**: Translator, not a dictionary — don't use as a vocab source. Easy to over-rely on.
- **Cost**: Free (limited) / paid (Pro).
- **For Juanpa**: Use only to **sanity-check** translations of his own Dutch output, not to look up words.

## C. Verb Conjugators

- **Link**: https://www.mijnwoordenboek.nl/werkwoord/
- **Level**: A0+.
- **Use case**: Full conjugation of any Dutch verb.
- **Strengths**: Free, instant, includes separable verbs and irregulars.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Default conjugation lookup.

### Cooljugator (Dutch)

- **Link**: https://cooljugator.com/nl
- **Level**: A0+.
- **Use case**: Conjugation + examples.
- **Strengths**: Pretty UI, free; mobile-friendly.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Backup to Mijnwoordenboek when needing English glosses inline.

## D. Pronunciation & Audio

### Forvo — Dutch

- **Link**: https://forvo.com/languages/nl/
- **Level**: any.
- **Use case**: Native speaker audio pronunciations of individual words.
- **Strengths**: Free; multiple speakers per word; often tagged NL vs BE.
- **Weaknesses**: Crowd-recorded — quality varies; some entries missing.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Use whenever IPA is unclear or to compare NL vs BE realisations.

### YouGlish — Dutch

- **Link**: https://youglish.com/pronounce/_/dutch
- **Level**: any.
- **Use case**: Hear any word/phrase used in YouTube videos in context.
- **Strengths**: Free; real-context pronunciation; allows filtering for region.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Daily 5-minute "how is *this word* really said?" exercise.

## E. Frequency Lists and Corpora

### Subtlex-NL

- **Link**: http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies/subtlex-nl
- **Level**: any.
- **Use case**: Word frequency list (~437k word types) derived from Dutch subtitles. Reference for vocabulary planning.
- **Strengths**: **Free**, downloadable as Excel/TSV; based on spoken-register subtitles; the gold standard for spoken-Dutch frequency (Keuleers, Brysbaert & New, 2010). Comparable across languages with Subtlex-ES and Subtlex-UK.
- **Weaknesses**: Subtitles bias toward dialogue.
- **Cost**: **Free**, requires registration link.
- **For Juanpa**: **Primary source for the app's vocabulary deck ordering.** Top 2,000 → A2; top 5,000 → B1; top 10,000 → B2 active+passive.

### CELEX2 (Dutch portion)

- **Link**: https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC96L14
- **Level**: research.
- **Use case**: Linguistic database — phonology, morphology, frequency.
- **Strengths**: Rich linguistic annotation.
- **Weaknesses**: **Paid** (LDC license) and academic-grade; overkill for learning.
- **Cost**: **Paid** (~hundreds USD for individuals).
- **For Juanpa**: Skip unless building advanced morphology features later.

### Corpus Gesproken Nederlands (CGN)

- **Link**: https://taalmaterialen.ivdnt.org/download/tstc-corpus-gesproken-nederlands/
- **Level**: research.
- **Use case**: ~9M-word corpus of spoken Dutch from NL and BE; transcribed and audio-aligned. Reference for what real spoken Dutch looks like.
- **Strengths**: **Free** (registration required); covers many registers; can be searched.
- **Weaknesses**: Heavy to download and query; requires CLARIN/Taalportaal login workflow.
- **Cost**: **Free** with academic-license registration.
- **For Juanpa**: Not for daily study. Useful at B1+ for the app to mine **reduction patterns** and authentic spoken examples.

### SoNaR Corpus

- **Link**: https://taalmaterialen.ivdnt.org/download/tstc-sonar-corpus/
- **Level**: research.
- **Use case**: 500M-word reference corpus of written Standard Dutch.
- **Cost**: **Free** with registration.
- **For Juanpa**: Reach-tool for advanced collocation queries.

### Open Dutch WordNet

- **Link**: https://github.com/cltl/OpenDutchWordnet
- **Level**: research / app-building.
- **Use case**: Semantic network of Dutch.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Possibly useful when the app needs synonym/antonym data offline.

## F. Grammar References Online

### Algemene Nederlandse Spraakkunst (ANS / e-ANS)

- **Link**: https://e-ans.ivdnt.org
- **Level**: B1+.
- **Use case**: Authoritative descriptive grammar of Dutch (in Dutch).
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive; canonical; **free**.
- **Weaknesses**: In Dutch — barrier below B1.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Reach-tool from B1; the *ANS* is the final word on any grammar question.

### Taaladvies — taaladvies.net

- **Link**: https://taaladvies.net
- **Level**: A2+ (Dutch).
- **Use case**: Practical "is this correct?" Q&A from the Taalunie.
- **Strengths**: **Free**, authoritative; covers spelling, choice between forms, *de/het*, idioms.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Specific-question lookup tool, not a course.

### Dutchgrammar.com

- **Link**: https://www.dutchgrammar.com
- **Level**: A0–B1, in English.
- **Use case**: Free English-medium reference grammar with exercises.
- **Strengths**: Free; well-structured; English explanations — perfect for Juanpa's bridge-language strategy.
- **Weaknesses**: UI dated; no audio.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: **Primary grammar reference** A0–B1.

### Zichtbaar Nederlands — *zichtbaarnederlands.nl*

- **Link**: https://www.zichtbaarnederlands.nl
- **Level**: A0–B1.
- **Use case**: Vocabulary by image and topic.
- **Strengths**: Free, illustrated, multilingual interface (incl. ES).
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **For Juanpa**: Vocabulary stepping-stone for concrete nouns.

## Methodology Rules for Juanpa

1. **One bilingual + one monolingual dictionary open at all times** when studying. Bilingual = Wiktionary (or Reverso). Monolingual = Woorden.org → later Van Dale/ANW.
2. **Always click the audio** on Wiktionary or paste into Forvo for any new word.
3. **Subtlex-NL frequency rank** decides whether a new word goes in the active SRS deck or just a passive list.
4. **Never use Google Translate** as a dictionary; prefer DeepL only for output sanity-checking.
5. **Always copy *de/het* + plural** when adding a noun card.
6. **Use Linguee** to choose between near-synonyms in context.
7. **Switch to monolingual lookups** by mid-B1 to break Spanish/English dependency.

## App Requirements Extracted from This Research

- Offline dictionary panel inside the app, sourced from **Wiktionary NL dumps** (CC-BY-SA) covering: lemma, IPA, audio link, *de/het*, plural, diminutive, conjugation, English gloss, Spanish gloss, etymology.
- **Subtlex-NL frequency rank** stored on every lemma; used to sort the new-card queue and to pick reading-text difficulty.
- **One-click "Open in Linguee / Forvo / YouGlish"** buttons on every word card.
- **Etymology indicator** (Germanic vs Latinate) per word, derived from Etymologiebank or Wiktionary etymology field — feeds the cognate-recognition module.
- **Audio caching**: pull Wiktionary commons audio at card creation, store locally, fall back to TTS if missing.
- **DeepL output-check integration** (optional, paid API) for Juanpa's free writing exercises — but **only at review time, not lookup time**, to avoid translator dependency.
- **Spelling/de-het authority check** against the *Woordenlijst Nederlandse Taal* dataset.
- **Source citation** in every dictionary entry the app shows, so the user can click through to the authoritative reference.
