## Resource Evaluation Criteria

When adding a resource, rate it on:
- **CEFR level range** — What level is it appropriate for?
- **Skill** — Vocabulary, grammar, listening, reading, speaking, writing?
- **Format** — Video, audio, text, interactive?
- **Cost** — Free / paid / freemium?
- **Quality rating** — 1–5 stars (personal assessment).
- **Notes** — Anything specific about why it is useful for Juanpa.
