# B2 Exam Resources

## Purpose

Provide the evidence and resources Juanpa needs to (a) decide which B2 Dutch exam to take, and (b) prepare for it. Two main candidates: **Staatsexamen NT2 Programma II** (Netherlands) and **CNaVT** (Belgium, KU Leuven). A third, **Certificaat Nederlands B2 (CvA / Goethe-style)**, is rarely used and skipped here.

## 2. Staatsexamen NT2 Programma II — Details

### What it is

- Official Dutch-as-a-second-language state exam in the Netherlands.
- **Programma I** ≈ B1 (vocational/lower professional contexts); **Programma II** ≈ B2 (academic/higher professional).
- Required for non-EU applicants to most Dutch universities and many regulated professions (medicine, law, education).

### Format (Programma II)

| Component | Duration | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Reading (*Lezen*) | 110 min | Multiple-choice and short-answer questions on multiple authentic texts (news, opinion, professional). |
| Listening (*Luisteren*) | 100 min | Multiple-choice on audio fragments: monologues, dialogues, interviews, lectures. |
| Writing (*Schrijven*) | 130 min | Several short tasks (filling forms, notes) + 1–2 longer essays/reports. |
| Speaking (*Spreken*) | 35 min | Computer-recorded responses to prompts: short answers, description, opinion, presentation. Note: **not** an interview with a human examiner. |

### Pass criteria

- Each component is scored on a 200–800 scale; **500 = pass** per component.
- All four components must be passed (can be retaken individually).

### Cost & registration

- €45 per component (2025); €180 if all four.
- Register via **DUO** (Dienst Uitvoering Onderwijs): https://duo.nl/particulier/staatsexamen-nt2/
- Sittings: most weeks year-round, at locations in NL (Zwolle, Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht, Eindhoven, Rijswijk).
- **Cannot be sat outside the Netherlands.** This is the decisive constraint for Juanpa.

### Official practice material

- DUO publishes recent past papers and answer keys: https://duo.nl/particulier/staatsexamen-nt2/voorbereiden.jsp
- *Voorbeeldexamens* (sample exams) for Programma II — free PDFs, audio downloads. **Essential**.
- CvTE syllabus and *Vaststellingen* (criteria): https://www.staatsexamensnt2.nl

### Commercial NT2 II prep materials

- *Vooruit!* (Coutinho) — NT2 method.
- *Op Niveau* — exam prep book (Boom NT2).
- *Nederlands in Gang* / *Nederlands in Actie* / *Nederlands op Niveau* (Boom Uitgevers) — staged A1→B2 series.
- *De Sprong* — exam-focused prep (B1→B2).
- *Examenidioom NT2* — vocabulary for the exam.

## 3. CNaVT — Details

### What it is

- *Certificaat Nederlands als Vreemde Taal* — Dutch as a Foreign Language certificate issued by **KU Leuven** under the Nederlandse Taalunie agreement (joint NL–BE language body).
- **Six profiles** mapped to CEFR:
  - PTIT — *Toeristisch en Informeel Taalgebruik* — A2
  - PMT — *Maatschappelijk Taalvaardig* — B1
  - PPT — *Professioneel Taalvaardig* — B1/B2
  - PTHO — *Educatief Startbekwaam* — **B2** (the main pre-university profile)
  - PAT — *Educatief Professioneel* — C1
- The B2 profile aimed at someone wanting to study at a Dutch-language university is **PTHO / *Educatief Startbekwaam***.

### Format (PTHO, B2)

| Component | Duration | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Reading & Listening | ~2 h combined | Authentic texts and audio about academic/educational contexts; comprehension questions. |
| Writing | ~2 h | Two writing tasks (e.g., summary, structured argument). |
| Speaking | ~30 min | Recorded responses or short interview, depending on centre. |

### Pass criteria

- Each skill scored separately; partial certification possible (pass some skills, retake others).
- Pass ≈ 60% per skill.

### Cost & registration

- **€85–€120** depending on the exam centre (centres set local prices).
- One exam window per year, **first/second week of May**; registration closes **mid-March**.
- Register via your local exam centre. List of centres: https://cnavt.org/examencentra

### Exam centres relevant to Juanpa (Colombia)

- As of recent years, CNaVT has had centres in **Bogotá** (Universidad Nacional / Universidad de los Andes, varies by year — verify yearly) and broader Latin America (Buenos Aires, São Paulo, Mexico City, Santiago).
- **Action item for Juanpa**: confirm which Colombian centre runs the exam in spring of the year he targets. If none, the closest alternatives are typically Mexico City or São Paulo.

### Official practice material

- KU Leuven CNaVT site: https://cnavt.org
- **Free past exams** for all profiles, including PTHO, with audio + answer keys: https://cnavt.org/oefenexamens
- *Praktische gids* for each profile (free PDF).

## 4. Other Considerations

### Inburgering / Civic Integration Exam

- The Dutch civic integration exam includes a language part (A2 then B1) — irrelevant for Juanpa unless he immigrates to NL. Skip.

### TUL / TUS — University-internal entrance tests

- Some Dutch universities run their own internal Dutch entrance test as an alternative to NT2 II. Out of scope unless Juanpa applies to a specific NL programme.

### IELTS / TOEFL analogy

- There is no "IELTS-of-Dutch" with global availability. Recognition for academic purposes goes through either NT2 II (NL-side) or CNaVT (BE-side).

## 6. Decision Framework for Juanpa

Use this decision tree:

1. **Does Juanpa intend to study or work in the Netherlands in the next ~3 years?**
   - **Yes** → NT2 II Programma II. Plan a 1–2 week trip to NL for the exam.
   - **No** → continue.
2. **Does Juanpa need a recognised certificate for any specific purpose?**
   - **University admission, scholarship, employer** → CNaVT *Educatief Startbekwaam* (B2). Verify the institution accepts it (they usually do).
   - **Personal milestone only** → CNaVT is cheaper and sittable in Colombia/LATAM; still the better choice.
3. **Default**: **CNaVT *Educatief Startbekwaam*** in the May window of the target year.

## 7. Methodology Rules for Juanpa

1. **Decide exam target by end of Phase 1**; this drives the curriculum's writing genres and listening sources from B1 onward.
2. **Practice with the actual exam format from B1 onward**, not only at the end.
3. **Time every practice attempt** — exam pacing is itself a skill.
4. **Mock exam every 4 weeks** from B1.
5. **Vocabulary deck must include exam topic clusters**: education, work, society, science, environment, health, technology, immigration, culture.
6. **Writing tasks must be graded against the published exam rubric**, not vibes.
7. **Listening practice at B1→B2 must include exam-style multi-question audios** (≥3 min, multi-speaker, accent variation).
8. **Speaking practice (CNaVT)** simulates recorded responses without live partners; train into a microphone with a timer.
9. **Register for the exam early** — CNaVT closes registration in March for the May window.

## 8. App Requirements Extracted from This Research

- **Exam-mode toggle**: lessons can be filtered to "NT2 II prep" or "CNaVT PTHO prep" tracks at B1+.
- **Mock-exam module** that mirrors the chosen exam's format and timing exactly (reading, listening, writing, speaking).
- **Speaking practice with recording + playback**, since both NT2 II and CNaVT speaking are recorded — train under the same conditions.
- **Writing rubric scoring** based on published NT2/CNaVT criteria (text type, structure, vocabulary range, grammar, register). Even rule-based scoring is valuable; add LLM-based evaluation as an option.
- **Topic-cluster vocabulary decks** aligned with B2 exam themes: education, work, society, science, environment, health, technology, immigration, culture.
- **Pacing trainer**: practice timed tasks with countdown.
- **Listening exercises with multi-question prompts** like exam format.
- **Reading exercises** using authentic NL/BE newspaper text excerpts with comprehension questions.
- **Progress dashboard** scored on the four skills separately, mirroring the exam's partial-certification logic.
