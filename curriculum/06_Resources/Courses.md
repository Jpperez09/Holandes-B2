# Dutch Courses, Channels, Podcasts, and Tutor Platforms

## Purpose

Evaluated catalogue of structured Dutch learning resources (courses, YouTube channels, podcasts, speaking platforms). Each entry: name, link, level, use case, strengths, weaknesses, cost, and how Juanpa should use it.

---

## A. Structured Courses — Free or Cheap

### Language Transfer — *Complete Dutch*

- **Link**: https://www.languagetransfer.org/dutch
- **Level**: A0 → mid-A2 (concept-level).
- **Use case**: Audio-only introduction that teaches Dutch by triggering pattern recognition through guided thinking, English as bridge.
- **Strengths**: **Free** (donation-based); ~90 episodes; extremely English-friendly; builds intuition for word order, modals, verb stems. Pavlik's method explicitly leverages a learner's existing English knowledge — perfect for Juanpa.
- **Weaknesses**: No reading/writing; no vocabulary breadth; stops well short of B2; no exercises.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: **Run from week 1, one episode per commute or walk**. Re-listen to weak episodes. Treat it as the conceptual onboarding for Dutch grammar, not the main course.

### Dutchgrammar.com

- **Link**: https://www.dutchgrammar.com
- **Level**: A0–B1.
- **Use case**: Free English-medium reference grammar with exercises.
- **Strengths**: Free; structured; thorough explanations in English; printable.
- **Weaknesses**: No audio; dated UI; not a "course," more a reference + drills.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Companion to whatever main course; one chapter per week aligned to current grammar focus.

### NedBox (KU Leuven / CTO)

- **Link**: https://www.nedbox.be
- **Level**: A2–B2.
- **Use case**: Free online practice with authentic Belgian Dutch content — news clips, songs, soap fragments, exercises.
- **Strengths**: **Free**; CEFR-tagged; aligned with the CNaVT exam philosophy; multimedia.
- **Weaknesses**: Flemish-leaning audio; UI dated; not a linear course.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Three sessions/week from A2 onward; primary listening source if targeting CNaVT.

### Naar Nederland / Oefenen.nl / Nt2taalmenu.nl

- **Link**: https://www.naarnederland.nl, https://oefenen.nl, https://nt2taalmenu.nl
- **Level**: A0–B1 (the NT2 free track stops short of full B2).
- **Use case**: Free government-supported practice for the integration exam and broader NT2 skills.
- **Strengths**: **Free**; varied; covers integration/society vocabulary.
- **Weaknesses**: Civic-integration register; not B2 in scope.
- **Cost**: **Free**.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Use *nt2taalmenu.nl* exercise bank as a supplementary drill source A1–B1.

### Duolingo Dutch

- **Link**: https://www.duolingo.com (NL course from English).
- **Level**: A0 → low A2 (claims B1 but realistically caps at A2).
- **Strengths**: Habit formation; free tier viable; gamified; Spanish-from-NL course also exists if reverse practice desired.
- **Weaknesses**: Very weak on speaking, writing, grammar explanation; sentence-translation-only model; not enough to reach B2 alone (consensus across language-learning communities; see Vesselinov & Grego 2012 efficacy study which targeted Spanish/Italian, not Dutch).
- **Cost**: Free / paid (€7/month).
- **How Juanpa should use it**: **Optional 5–10 min/day** for habit reinforcement only; never the main course. Don't pay.

### Memrise — Dutch courses

- **Link**: https://www.memrise.com
- **Level**: A0–B1.
- **Use case**: Vocabulary SRS with native-speaker video clips.
- **Strengths**: Real video of native speakers saying phrases — useful for listening.
- **Weaknesses**: Free tier reduced over time; vocabulary lists vary in quality.
- **Cost**: Free / paid.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Use the **video phrases** feature specifically for pronunciation models; skip the SRS (we will use Anki/our app).

### Anki (with a quality Dutch deck)

- **Link**: https://apps.ankiweb.net
- **Level**: A0–C1.
- **Use case**: SRS vocabulary. Recommended deck: *Dutch 5000 Most Common Words (Subtlex-based)* on AnkiWeb.
- **Strengths**: **Free**, scientifically validated SRS; full control.
- **Weaknesses**: Setup overhead; quality varies by deck.
- **Cost**: **Free** (desktop, web, Android); paid iOS.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Until the project's own app handles SRS, use Anki with a Subtlex-NL frequency deck. Set new-card limit to 15–20/day.

## B. Structured Courses — Paid

### Bart de Pau — *Learn Dutch* (learndutch.org)

- **Link**: https://www.learndutch.org
- **Level**: A0–B2.
- **Use case**: YouTube videos (free) + paid online courses including *Het Grote Nederlandse Examenboek* for NT2 II.
- **Strengths**: Highest-quality free YouTube Dutch content (1000+ videos); clear, methodical; NT2-aligned; **paid courses are reasonably priced and exam-targeted**.
- **Weaknesses**: Heavy NL-Randstad accent (good for Juanpa's target). Paid courses sometimes oversell.
- **Cost**: Free (YouTube) / paid (online courses, €100–€300 ranges).
- **How Juanpa should use it**: **Free YouTube content from day 1**; consider buying the NT2 II prep course in B1 if exam-targeted.

### NT2 in-person courses at Dutch universities (taught remotely)

- **Universiteit van Amsterdam INTT**, **Universiteit Leiden Talencentrum**, **Universiteit Utrecht Babel** — many offer live online B1/B2 courses; semester-based; ~€500–€1,200.
- **Use case**: Structured weekly live classes; pairs well with NT2 II target.
- **Cost**: **Paid**, but high-quality.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Consider for the **B1→B2 bridge** if self-study plateaus. UvA INTT online B2 group course is well-regarded.

### Babbel Dutch

- **Link**: https://www.babbel.com
- **Level**: A0–B1.
- **Strengths**: Better than Duolingo for grammar; concise lessons.
- **Weaknesses**: Caps below B2; subscription required.
- **Cost**: ~€7/month annual.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Skip — Bart de Pau + Dutchgrammar.com cover the same ground for free.

### Drongo / Pippa Pas Op / Lingoda

- **Lingoda Dutch**: live group classes with certified teachers; €10–€20 per class; CEFR-tracked.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Possible substitute for live speaking practice from A2.

### Mondly / Pimsleur / Rosetta Stone Dutch

- All exist; quality and value variable.
- **Pimsleur Dutch** has a solid reputation for early listening/speaking habituation (audio-only, ~30 lessons), but coverage is shallow.
- **How Juanpa should use it**: Pimsleur is optional A0–A1 supplement; Mondly/Rosetta Stone skip.

**Recommended cadence for Juanpa**:
- A0–A1: **No tutor yet.** Build phonology and 500–1,000 vocab first; tutor time is wasted at zero.
- A2: **1× iTalki tutor session per week** (45 min). Use a tutor who works in English bridge if possible.
- B1: **2× per week**; mix tutor (corrective) with **Tandem partner** (free conversation).
- B2: **2–3× per week** including exam-targeted speaking practice.

## Methodology Rules for Juanpa

1. **Pick ONE main course per phase**, not three. Stacking courses creates overlap and decision fatigue.
2. **Pair the main course with one passive-listening source** that runs in the background (commute, chores).
3. **Always start a phase by re-doing the previous phase's hardest 10%**, not from scratch.
4. **Resources must serve a phase, not impress you** — drop anything that doesn't match the current CEFR target.
5. **Free first, paid only when free has been exhausted** — there's enough free Dutch content to reach B2.
6. **One paid resource max at a time** to keep cost discipline.
7. **Audio every day**, even on rest-grammar days.
8. **Tutor only after week 12** (A2 onset); earlier is wasteful.
9. **Discard apps after 4 weeks** if not delivering measurable CEFR-relevant gains.

## App Requirements Extracted from This Research

- **External-resource library** inside the app: curated, level-tagged links to Bart de Pau, NT2 Met Sterre, NedBox, NOS, NRC, podcasts, etc., filterable by CEFR level.
- **Daily playlist generator**: pick one podcast episode + one YouTube video + one reading text matched to Juanpa's current level and weakest skill.
- **Resource progression tracking**: mark a resource "completed" or "owned" so the app stops surfacing it.
- **Embedded transcript display** for selected free podcasts where transcripts exist (e.g., NPO sometimes publishes).
- **Anki bridge / SRS layer** — until the app's SRS is fully featured, import/export with Anki .apkg format. Source decks from Subtlex-NL.
- **Tutor-prep prompt generator**: before every iTalki lesson, the app generates 5 talking points and 10 target vocabulary items based on the week's content — maximizes the value of paid speaking time.
- **Speaking-self-record module** with playback comparison against a model audio (for shadowing and exam speaking).
- **Listening curriculum**: a queue of podcasts and clips at the right level, with replay-at-slower-speed toggle.
- **Reading curriculum**: leveled text recommendations from Wablieft → NOS → NRC, with click-to-define gloss.
- **Cost ledger**: the app tracks any subscriptions Juanpa adds so he can audit total spend.
