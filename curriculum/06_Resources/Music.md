## Tips for Using Music in Language Learning

- Music is best for phonology and rhythm, not grammar.
- Use lyrics (teksten) to follow along and look up vocabulary.
- Re-listen to familiar songs — the repetition helps.
- Prefer songs with clear diction over heavily auto-tuned or fast-rapped Dutch.
