# Level 013 — Adjective endings: *de grote man, een groot huis*

## Purpose

Adjectives before a noun and the *-e* rule, with colours, size and temperature and the words for man, woman and boy. It makes descriptions possible for the café, the home and the daily routine.

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-013_Adjective_Endings]] are checked.
- [ ] Adds the right ending in at least 10 of 12 phrases.
- [ ] Describes five objects aloud with a colour or a size.
