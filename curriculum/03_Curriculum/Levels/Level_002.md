# Level 002 — Dutch Sounds and Greetings

## Purpose

Consolidate Dutch phonology and add productive greeting + introduction language. After Level 002, Juanpa can greet a Dutch speaker, say who he is, and respond to a greeting in fixed phrases. This is the **first conversation**.

## Exit Criteria

Juanpa can advance to Level 003 when:

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-002_Dutch_Sounds_and_Greetings]] are checked.
- [ ] He can pronounce the four phonemes from Level 001 in 3 cued words each, self-rated ≥ 3/5.
- [ ] He can produce 5 greetings/farewell phrases without looking at the text.
- [ ] He has recorded one 30-second greeting interaction (himself + an imagined Dutch speaker).
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20 (cards from Level 001 + Level 002 vocabulary).

## Listening Targets

- Hear 5 greetings/farewell phrases at slow-natural speed.
- Hear numbers 0–10 pronounced.
- Hear one 60-second slow-Dutch monologue introducing a person.

## Speaking Targets

- Produce 5 greetings/farewell phrases out loud.
- Count from 1 to 10 in Dutch.
- Record a 30-second simulated greeting + self-introduction (your name + country).

## Reading Targets

- Read a 60–80 word Dutch text consisting of a person introducing themselves.

## Writing Targets

- Write 4 sentences introducing yourself (name, country, "I speak Spanish/English").
- Free-form: 2–3 sentences in [[../../04_Daily_Logs/]] in Dutch (with English assists if needed).

## Pronunciation Targets

- Phonemes from Level 001 carried over and drilled in new lexical contexts.
- New focus: word stress in greeting phrases (`goedemórgen`, `goedeavond`).
- New focus: schwa reduction (do not over-articulate `de`, `het`, `een`).

## Real-World Task

> **"Greet someone in Dutch and respond to a Dutch greeting."**
> Channel: a Dutch learner Discord, Tandem app message, or a video call greeting. Self-report deliverable: a 30-second voice memo or a 2-line text exchange.
