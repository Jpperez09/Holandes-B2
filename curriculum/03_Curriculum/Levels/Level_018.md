# Level 018 — Making plans with *gaan* + infinitive

## Purpose

Plans and the near future with *gaan* + infinitive, with *naar*, time words and *misschien*. It closes the October block by joining modals, separable verbs, time and places.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-018_Making_Plans]] are checked.
- [ ] Rewrites at least 10 of 12 sentences as plans with the infinitive at the end.
- [ ] Tells and asks about plans for the weekend and a holiday aloud.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
