# Level 007 — Numbers 11–100, age and prices

## Purpose

Numbers beyond ten, so the learner can give an age, read a price and follow dates and times in the next modules.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-007_Numbers_11_100_Age_and_Prices]] are checked.
- [ ] Writes 10 numbers between 12 and 99 in words with at most one error.
- [ ] Gives an age and three prices in full Dutch sentences.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
