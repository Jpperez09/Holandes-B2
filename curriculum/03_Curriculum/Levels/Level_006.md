# Level 006 — Negation: *niet* vs *geen*

## Purpose

First module of the October block. It consolidates the negation introduced in MOD-005 (*niet* and *geen*) and adds *nooit*, *niets* and *niemand*, so that every later module can use negative sentences naturally.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-006_Negation_Niet_vs_Geen]] are checked.
- [ ] Chooses *niet* or *geen* correctly in at least 10 of 12 sentences.
- [ ] Answers three yes/no questions with a full negative sentence.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
