# Level 011 — This and that: *deze, die, dit, dat*

## Purpose

Pointing at things and comparing them: *deze / die* and *dit / dat*, with clothes and objects and the words for new, expensive and cheap.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-011_This_and_That_Deze_Die_Dit_Dat]] are checked.
- [ ] Chooses deze / die / dit / dat correctly in at least 11 of 12 gaps.
- [ ] Compares five things aloud, near and far, with prices.
