# Level 014 — At the café: *ik wil graag…, mag ik…*

## Purpose

The first real-life situation of the October block: ordering at a café with polite requests, *met / zonder / nog*, and the bill. It joins modals, numbers and prices.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-014_At_the_Cafe]] are checked.
- [ ] Orders a drink and a snack politely and asks for the bill without help.
- [ ] Completes 11 of 12 café sentences correctly.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
