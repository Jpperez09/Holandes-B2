# Level 012 — Modal verbs: *kunnen, willen, moeten, mogen*

## Purpose

Modal verbs: ability, wish, obligation and permission, with the infinitive at the end of the clause. It opens the way to polite requests (*Mag ik…?*) and to plans (*gaan* + infinitive).

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-012_Modal_Verbs]] are checked.
- [ ] Conjugates the four modals in the present tense with at most two errors.
- [ ] Says 5 sentences with a modal and the infinitive at the end.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
