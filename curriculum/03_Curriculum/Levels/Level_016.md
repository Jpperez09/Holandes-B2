# Level 016 — Prepositions of place and *er is / er zijn*

## Purpose

Saying where things are and what there is: prepositions of place, the verbs *staan / zitten / is* and *er is / er zijn*. It prepares the daily routine (home, shops) and plans.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-016_Places_and_Er_Is_Er_Zijn]] are checked.
- [ ] Chooses the right preposition in at least 11 of 12 gaps.
- [ ] Describes a room aloud with five prepositions and *er is / er zijn*.
