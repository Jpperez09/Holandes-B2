# Level 015 — Irregular plurals and diminutives (*-je*)

## Purpose

Irregular plurals and diminutives, the two ways Dutch nouns most often surprise a learner. It makes the words of everyday life (*kopje, broodje, kaartje, beetje*) available.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-015_Irregular_Plurals_and_Diminutives]] are checked.
- [ ] Gives the plural of at least 11 of 12 nouns.
- [ ] Makes 8 diminutives with the right article and suffix with at most one error.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
