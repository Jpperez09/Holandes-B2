# Level 001 — First Contact

## Purpose

Open the curriculum. Juanpa goes from "no Dutch" to "I have heard Dutch, I can produce a few sounds, I have written my name in Dutch." This is the **affective on-ramp**: lower the cost of starting tomorrow.

The hidden goal: confirm the daily loop works (open app → open module → finish in 30–45 minutes → close).

## Exit Criteria

Juanpa can advance to Level 002 when:

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-001_First_Contact]] are checked.
- [ ] He has heard the four target Dutch phonemes pronounced: `/x/`, `/ɛi/`, `/œy/`, schwa.
- [ ] He has spoken his name and one greeting in Dutch out loud (self-rated, not perfect).
- [ ] He has written 5 Dutch words by hand or in the daily log.
- [ ] He has zero overdue SRS cards (trivially true — there are none yet).

## Vocabulary Targets

- New lemmas this level: **~10** (greetings + 3–4 grammar function words).
- All items come from [[../../05_Exercises/Generated/Vocabulary_Seed_A0_A1]] tagged with `module: MOD-001`.
- Cognate density target: **≥ 60%** (intentionally high to lower friction).

## Grammar Targets

No grammar pattern is introduced productively at Level 001. Patterns from [[../../05_Exercises/Generated/Grammar_Patterns_A0_A1]] that are **previewed** (recognized but not drilled):

- `dit-is-dat-is`
- `ik-heet`

Juanpa is exposed to these as fixed chunks, not analyzed structures.

## Listening Targets

- Hear the Dutch alphabet pronounced clearly.
- Hear the four target phonemes (`/x/`, `/ɛi/`, `/œy/`, schwa) in 3+ example words each.
- Hear 5 basic greetings spoken at slow-natural speed.

## Speaking Targets

- Produce his own name in Dutch out loud (recording optional).
- Repeat 5 greetings after a model. Self-rating only.

> No monologue required at Level 1. See [[../../02_Methodology/Juanpa_Methodology_V1]] for the staged speaking introduction.

## Reading Targets

- Read a 30–50 word Dutch text using only the day-1 vocabulary.
- Recognize the printed forms of the four target phonemes in spelling: `g/ch`, `ij/ei`, `ui`, `e` (unstressed).

## Writing Targets

- Write own name and country in Dutch.
- Write 3 Dutch words (chosen from the module vocabulary).

## Pronunciation Targets

| Phoneme | Spelling | Example | Spanish-L1 trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/x/` | `g`, `ch` | `goedemorgen`, `acht` | More uvular than Spanish `jota`; do not substitute. |
| `/ɛi/` | `ij`, `ei` | `zijn`, `klein` | No Spanish equivalent — drill as new sound. |
| `/œy/` | `ui` | `huis`, `uit` | Rounded front diphthong; not `oo` or `uy`. |
| schwa `/ə/` | unstressed `e` | `de`, `een` | Spanish has no schwa; do not pronounce as `/e/`. |

## Real-World Task

> **"Tell one Dutch speaker (or one Dutch learner Discord/Tandem/iTalki contact) your name and country in Dutch."**
> Acceptable verification: a screenshot of the text message, a voice memo, or a self-report in [[../../04_Daily_Logs/]].
