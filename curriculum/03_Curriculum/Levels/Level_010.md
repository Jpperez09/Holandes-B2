# Level 010 — Verb stems, spelling rules and *gaan / staan / doen / zien*

## Purpose

The regular present tense in full: finding the stem, the spelling rules that keep the sound, and the four short verbs *gaan, staan, doen, zien*. It is the base for modals, separable verbs and plans.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-010_Verb_Stems_and_Spelling]] are checked.
- [ ] Gives the *ik* and *hij* forms of 12 verbs with at most two errors.
- [ ] Uses at least six different verb forms correctly in a spoken description.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
