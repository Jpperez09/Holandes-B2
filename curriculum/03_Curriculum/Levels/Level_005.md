# Level 005 — Basic Sentences and Questions

## Purpose

First exposure to **V2 word order** — the defining feature of Dutch syntax. After Level 005, Juanpa can produce simple statements and yes/no questions with the finite verb in second position. He can also produce one-word *wh-* questions.

This level closes the **Foundation micro-block (Levels 1–5)** by tying phonology + identity + nouns + V2 into a coherent first end-to-end Dutch competence: *Hallo, ik ben Juan. Ik kom uit Colombia. Ik heb een broer. Mijn broer is leraar. Waar woon jij?*

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-005_Basic_Sentences_and_Questions]] are checked.
- [ ] Juanpa can produce 5 sentences with adverb fronting and correct V2 (e.g. *Vandaag ben ik moe* — not *Vandaag ik ben moe*).
- [ ] He can form 5 yes/no questions by verb inversion (`Ben jij…?`, `Heb jij…?`).
- [ ] He can form 5 *wh-*questions using `wie / wat / waar / wanneer / hoe`.
- [ ] He records a 90-second self-introduction tying together everything from Levels 1–5.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.

## Grammar Targets

| Pattern slug | Stage |
|---|---|
| `v2-main-clause` | Drilled — finite verb in 2nd position, regardless of what fronts. |
| `question-yes-no` | Drilled — subject-verb inversion: `[V-fin] [subject] [rest]?`. |
| `question-word` | Drilled — `wie / wat / waar / wanneer / hoe / hoeveel` + V2. |
| `present-tense-regular` | Drilled — stem + `-t` (jij/hij/zij/u) / `-en` (wij/jullie/zij). |

## Listening Targets

- Three 60–90 second dialogues with adverb-fronted statements and questions. Identify the finite verb position in 10 sentences.
- One 90-second monologue introducing a Dutch person, peppered with statements and questions.

## Speaking Targets

- 90-second monologue: integrate Levels 1–5 vocabulary + grammar.
- Q&A drill (recorded): form 5 yes/no questions and 5 *wh-*questions on the fly from cued prompts.

## Reading Targets

- 150-word Dutch dialogue between two Dutch speakers introducing themselves.
- Mark every finite verb position in the text.

## Writing Targets

- Write a 6–8 sentence Dutch dialogue (you + an imagined interlocutor). Must include:
  - At least 2 adverb-fronted statements.
  - At least 2 yes/no questions.
  - At least 2 *wh-*questions.

## Pronunciation Targets

- Question intonation contour (rising on yes/no; falling on *wh-*questions).
- All previously-introduced phonemes carried over.
- New target: short vs long vowel contrast in `man/maan, bos/boos, vis/vies` (preview only; full mastery in Level 008+).
