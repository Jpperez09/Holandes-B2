# Level 004 — Nouns: De / Het and Core Objects

## Purpose

Introduce the Dutch noun system. Every Dutch noun lives with its article — **de** (common gender) or **het** (neuter) — and the article must be learned with the noun, never separately (D-008 in [[../../00_Project/DECISION_REGISTER_V1]]). This level is the moment Juanpa stops thinking of *huis* as "house" and starts thinking of it as *het huis*.

Secondary: *hebben* (to have) is introduced, which lets Juanpa say things like *ik heb een hond* ("I have a dog").

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-004_Nouns_De_Het_and_Core_Objects]] are checked.
- [ ] Juanpa correctly identifies the article for **at least 25 of the 30 nouns** introduced (recognition test).
- [ ] He produces 5 *ik heb een…* sentences and 5 *dit/dat is de/het…* sentences.

## Vocabulary Targets

- New lemmas: **~30** (concrete nouns: home, body, food/drink core, family expansion). Article-tagged.
- Cumulative: **~90 lemmas**.
- de/het balance for this batch: roughly 70% `de`, 30% `het` (matches Dutch corpus distribution per Donaldson 2008).
- Source: [[../../05_Exercises/Generated/Vocabulary_Seed_A0_A1]] tagged `module: MOD-004`.

## Grammar Targets

| Pattern slug | Stage |
|---|---|
| `de-het-article` | Drilled — definite article assignment must be learned per noun. |
| `present-tense-zijn-hebben` | Drilled — `hebben` full conjugation. |
| `plural-formation` | Drilled — `-en` (most nouns) vs `-s` (loanwords + open-syllable nouns). |
| `noun-phrase-een` | Drilled — indefinite article *een*. |

## Listening Targets

- Two 60–90 second clips of someone describing their home/room. Find 5 nouns + their articles.
- Hear `ik heb / jij hebt / hij heeft` in natural context.

## Speaking Targets

- Article drill (recorded): say 20 nouns aloud, each preceded by its correct article.
- 60-second monologue: describe your room using `er is/zijn …` (introduced as a fixed chunk) or `mijn kamer heeft …`.

## Reading Targets

- 120-word Dutch text describing a fictional Dutch house and family. Identify every noun's article in the text.

## Writing Targets

- Write 8 sentences using vocabulary from MOD-004 with correct articles.
- Include at least 3 *ik heb een…* sentences and 3 *dit/dat is de/het…* sentences.

## Pronunciation Targets

- Consonant clusters: `kn-` (`knie`), `schr-` (`schrijven` — recognition).
- Diphthong `/ʌu/` (`mouw`, `koud`) — newly added to the phonemic palette.
- Distinction between `de` (`/də/`, schwa) and `dé` (`/de/`, stressed contrastive) — explicitly contrasted.
