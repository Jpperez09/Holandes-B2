# Level 003 — I Am, You Are

## Purpose

First real Dutch grammar: the present tense of *zijn* (to be). After Level 003, Juanpa can construct his own simple identity sentences instead of repeating memorized chunks. The chunk *ik ben* becomes a productive `[subject] + [zijn-form]` pattern.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-003_I_Am_You_Are]] are checked.
- [ ] Juanpa can fill in `___ ben`, `jij ___`, `hij ___`, `wij ___` correctly from memory.
- [ ] He can produce 5 original Dutch sentences using *zijn* (e.g. *ik ben Colombiaans, mijn moeder is leraar*).
- [ ] He recorded a 60-second self-introduction using `ik ben` + 3 properties.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.

## Listening Targets

- Three 60–90 second dialogues of two Dutch speakers introducing each other.
- Hear `zijn` conjugated across all six persons in natural context.

## Speaking Targets

- Conjugation drill (recorded): say all six forms of *zijn* with subject pronouns.
- 60-second self-introduction monologue.
- Cued production: 5 sentences of the form *[subject] + [form of zijn] + [property]*.

## Reading Targets

- 100-word Dutch text: a short profile of a fictional person (name, country, language, job).
- Find and underline every form of *zijn* in the text.

## Writing Targets

- Write your own profile (5–8 sentences) using `ik ben`, `mijn naam is`, `ik kom uit`, `ik spreek`.
- Save to [[../../04_Daily_Logs/]].

## Pronunciation Targets

- Final devoicing in *Nederland → Nederlant*, *land → lant*.
- Stress patterns in nationality adjectives (`Cololmbiáans, Nederlánds`).
- Schwa carry-over from Level 002 (`Nederlandse` — the final `-e` is schwa).
