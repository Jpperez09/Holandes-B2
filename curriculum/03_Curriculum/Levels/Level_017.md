# Level 017 — Daily routine and separable verbs

## Purpose

The daily routine and separable verbs: the particle at the end of the clause, the verb whole with a modal, and the parts of the day. It prepares the plans of the last module.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-017_Daily_Routine_and_Separable_Verbs]] are checked.
- [ ] Writes 10 of 12 separable-verb sentences with the particle at the end.
- [ ] Tells a routine with at least five times and three separable verbs.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
