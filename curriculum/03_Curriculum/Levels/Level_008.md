# Level 008 — Time, days and months

## Purpose

Time, days and months, so the learner can arrange a meeting, say when something happens and read a simple schedule.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All checklist items in [[../Modules/MOD-008_Time_Days_and_Months]] are checked.
- [ ] Tells the time correctly for 9 of 10 clocks, including *half*.
- [ ] Names the seven days and twelve months in order.
- [ ] SRS overdue ≤ 20.
