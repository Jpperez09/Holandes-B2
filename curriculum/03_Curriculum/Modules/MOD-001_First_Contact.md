# MOD-001 — First Contact

**Module ID:** `MOD-001`
**Level:** 1
**CEFR band:** A0-A1 (Foundation)
**Estimated time:** 30 minutes
**Prerequisites:** none

---

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, Juanpa will be able to:

- Recognize four diagnostic Dutch phonemes (`/x/`, `/ɛi/`, `/œy/`, schwa) when he hears them.
- Pronounce his own name and country in Dutch out loud.
- Recognize 10 Dutch words on paper.
- Write 5 Dutch words by hand or in the daily log.

> No grammar pattern is taught at Level 1. The aim is **affective on-ramp** and phonological orientation.

## 5. Pronunciation

The single most important section of Level 1.

| Phoneme | Spelling | 3 example words | Spanish-L1 trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/x/` | `g`, `ch` | *goedemorgen*, *acht*, *graag* | More uvular than Spanish *jota*. |
| `/ɛi/` | `ij`, `ei` | *zijn*, *klein*, *mijn* | No Spanish equivalent. Drill explicitly. |
| `/œy/` | `ui` | *huis*, *uit*, *jullie* | Rounded front diphthong. Not `oo` or `uy`. |
| schwa `/ə/` | unstressed `e` | *de*, *een*, *Nederlandse* | Spanish has no schwa. Resist over-articulation. |

### Mini-drill (5 minutes)

1. Listen to a Dutch alphabet audio (recommended: [Bart de Pau — Dutch alphabet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eBuijTjmrLU) or any equivalent).
2. Repeat the four target phonemes 5× each.
3. Read the 10 vocabulary words aloud. Self-rate on the 1–5 rubric in §7.

### Self-rubric

| Criterion | 1 — Poor | 3 — OK | 5 — Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Said each phrase aloud at all | 0–1 phrases | 3 phrases | 5 phrases |
| /x/ produced | Substituted Spanish *jota* | Recognizable | Native-like |
| /ɛi/ produced | Said as *ei* like in Spanish *peine* | Diphthongal | Native-like |
| /œy/ produced | Said as *uy* like in Spanish *muy* | Diphthongal | Native-like |
| Schwa reduced | Over-articulated all *e* | Some schwas | Consistent schwa |

Pass: average ≥ 3/5.
