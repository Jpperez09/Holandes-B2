# MOD-101 — Weekly Review 1 (MOD-001 and MOD-002)

**Module ID:** `MOD-101`
**Level:** 2 (the last standard level this review covers)
**CEFR band:** A0-A1 (Foundation)
**Estimated time:** 30 minutes
**Type:** weekly review (Saturday). **No new vocabulary.** It never blocks the unlock chain.
**Covers:** [[MOD-001_First_Contact]], [[MOD-002_Dutch_Sounds_and_Greetings]]

---

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recall, without looking, the greetings, the numbers 0–10 and the words for introducing yourself.
- Use *Ik heet ___*, *Mijn naam is ___* and *Ik kom uit ___* without hesitating.
- Hear and produce the four target sounds `/x/`, `/ɛi/`, `/œy/` and schwa.
- Record one 45–60 second spoken introduction and write one short message, both from memory.

## 4. Grammar Recap (no new grammar)

### 4.1. `ik-heet` — saying your name

**Rule:** `[subject] + heten + [name]`. *Heten* is **not** reflexive (Spanish *me llamo* → Dutch *ik heet*, no *me*).

| Dutch | English |
|---|---|
| Ik heet Juan. | My name is Juan. |
| Hij heet Pieter. | He is called Pieter. |
| Zij heet Anna. | She is called Anna. |

Fixed chunks from MOD-001, used without analysing them yet: *Ik ben Juan.* / *Mijn naam is Juan Pablo.* / *Ik kom uit Colombia.* / *Ik leer Nederlands.*

## 5. Pronunciation

| Focus | Check yourself |
|---|---|
| `/x/` (g, ch) | Say *acht*, *goedemorgen*, *graag* three times. The sound is rougher than Spanish *j*. |
| `/ɛi/` (ij, ei) | Say *zijn*, *klein*, *mijn* three times. No Spanish equivalent. |
| `/œy/` (ui) | Say *huis*, *uit*, *jullie* three times. Rounded lips; not *oy*. |
| Schwa and stress | Say *goedemórgen*, *goedemíddag*, *tot zíens*: stress the second part, reduce every unstressed *e*. |

## 8. Listening Practice

### 8.1. Shadowing text (meeting a neighbour, about 30 words)

In the app, press ▶ (the whole text, or one line at a time). Outside the app, paste the Dutch below into your system voice. Listen once, then read it aloud *with* the voice (shadowing), twice.

```
Juan: Goedemiddag, mevrouw.
Mevrouw Bakker: Goedemiddag! Ik heet Bakker.
Juan: Ik heet Juan. Ik kom uit Colombia.
Mevrouw Bakker: Ik kom uit Nederland.
Juan: Ik leer Nederlands.
Mevrouw Bakker: Goed, Juan! Tot ziens!
Juan: Tot ziens, mevrouw. Dank u wel!
```

### 8.2. English

| Speaker | Dutch | English |
|---|---|---|
| Juan | Goedemiddag, mevrouw. | Good afternoon, madam. |
| Mevrouw Bakker | Goedemiddag! Ik heet Bakker. | Good afternoon! My name is Bakker. |
| Juan | Ik heet Juan. Ik kom uit Colombia. | My name is Juan. I come from Colombia. |
| Mevrouw Bakker | Ik kom uit Nederland. | I come from the Netherlands. |
| Juan | Ik leer Nederlands. | I am learning Dutch. |
| Mevrouw Bakker | Goed, Juan! Tot ziens! | Good, Juan! Goodbye! |
| Juan | Tot ziens, mevrouw. Dank u wel! | Goodbye, madam. Thank you! |

### 8.3. Check

- Where does Mevrouw Bakker come from? (*Nederland*)
- Which greeting do they use? Would you use the same at 8 p.m.? (*goedemiddag* → *goedenavond*)
- Underline every word with `/x/`, `/ɛi/` or `/œy/` in the Dutch text.

## 9. Speaking Practice

### 9.1. Recorded production

> **Introduce yourself to a new Dutch neighbour, as if she had just opened the door.**

Must include:
- A greeting that fits the time of day (*goedemorgen / goedemiddag / goedenavond*).
- *Ik heet ___.* and *Ik kom uit ___.*
- *Ik leer Nederlands.*
- Count from one to five aloud, then give a (made-up) house number.
- A thank-you (*dank u wel*) and a goodbye (*tot ziens*).

Record 45–60 seconds, without reading. Save as `04_Daily_Logs/recordings/MOD-101_YYYY-MM-DD.m4a`.

### 9.2. Self-assessment (1–5)

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sounds `/x/`, `/ɛi/`, `/œy/`, schwa | I mostly said Spanish sounds | Some were right | All four were clearly Dutch |
| Memory | I read nearly everything | I looked up two or three words | I did not look anything up |
| Completeness | Missing three or more items | Missing one or two | Everything in the list |

## 10. Writing Practice

> **Write a 5-sentence message that introduces you to a Dutch language partner.**

Must include:
- A greeting and *Ik heet ___.*
- *Ik kom uit ___.*
- *Ik leer Nederlands.*
- One number between 0 and 10 written as a word.
- A thank-you and a goodbye.

Save to `04_Daily_Logs/`. Read it aloud once; if a sentence feels wrong, check it against MOD-002 §4 before the quiz.
