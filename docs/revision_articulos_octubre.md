## Worth a second look

Not errors that I know of, but the choices a native speaker is most likely to want to confirm:

- **Gender of loan words:** *de lunch*, *de limonade*, *het café*, *het weekend*, *het horloge*, *de supermarkt*.
- **Diminutives** (all *het*): *meisje*, *jongetje*, *kopje*, *broodje*, *kaartje*, *beetje*.
